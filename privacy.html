Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Divija Enterprise ("we", "us", "our"), 60, Siddhivinayak Arcade, Near ICICI Bank, Odhav Circle, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 382415, India, provides domestic and international courier services. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website divijaenterprise.shop, our counter, phone calls, and our messages on WhatsApp.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile number, WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Shipment details: sender and receiver names, addresses, phone numbers, the contents, weight and value of shipments, and identity or customs documents where the law or the courier company requires them.
  • Messages: the messages, photos and documents you send us on WhatsApp, phone or email, and our replies.
  • Payment records: amounts paid and the payment method. We do not store full card or bank details.
  • Website: our website does not use its own cookies or tracking. Basic technical logs may be kept by our hosting provider.

2. How we use your information

  • To book, pick up, ship and deliver your courier and to give you tracking updates.
  • To answer your questions and give quotes, including through automated replies on WhatsApp.
  • To send booking confirmations, delivery updates and reminders about your shipments.
  • To send offers and updates about our services, only if you have agreed to receive them. You can stop these at any time (see section 5).
  • To keep records required by law, such as invoices and tax records, and to prevent fraud.

3. WhatsApp messages

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta Platforms, to talk with customers. When you message us on WhatsApp, your messages are handled by WhatsApp under WhatsApp's privacy policy, and by the customer messaging software we use to manage our conversations and enquiries. Some replies may be sent by an automated assistant; you can ask to talk to a person at any time.

4. Who we share information with

We share only what is needed to provide the service:

  • Courier companies that carry your shipment, such as Shree Mahavir Express Services, Trackon, DTDC and others, which need sender and receiver details to deliver it.
  • Customs and government authorities for international shipments or when the law requires it.
  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as WhatsApp (Meta), our customer messaging software, email and payment providers. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Stop marketing messages: reply STOP to any of our WhatsApp messages, or tell us by phone or email. You will still receive updates about shipments you have booked.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw consent you gave us at any time; this does not affect anything done before.

These rights are provided under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law. Contact us using the details below to use them.

6. How long we keep information

We keep shipment and payment records for as long as required by tax and other laws (generally up to 8 years). We keep messages and enquiry details for as long as needed to serve you and then delete them or keep them in a form that no longer identifies you.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will inform you and the authorities of a data breach as required by law.

8. Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
